Lana Lux

Lana Lux is an artist, game developer, and creative technologist making interactive experiences. through 3D art, code, live broadcasting, performance, and a range of other media and technologies.

Her current project, STRAIN, is a single-player survival game set in a strange, character-driven world.

Lana is interested in the ways technology can create space for curiosity, learning, and connection.
